## The Takko Dialect

The local dialect section is a little different, though. It's my attempt at preserving something that has been slowly fading as the older generation passes it on to the younger ones less and less. Young people rarely speak it.

Takko dialect isn't completely unique to Takko. It's a local flavor of Nanbu-ben, which is spoken throughout the region and has been documented by others, but some parts of it are only spoken in Takko. On top of that, even different areas inside Takko, like Kamigo, speak a little differently from central Takko.

## English Tools and Games

The other half of my website is a playground for ideas and a collection of games and tools I use in class and to make lessons. Since I started sharing them, many other English teachers in Japan have started using them too!

My website has become something I use in almost every class. One day, I just got sick of tools and games that were a little off, were missing some polish or a setting I really wanted, or cost money. So I took it into my own hands and started building games and tools that fit my needs, and they're all free! There's a timer that looks just like the one in class, a bunch of worksheet makers that save me hours on Canva, a quick-reference page for Japanese phrases I want to use in class but always forget, and tons of games, like my ABC game, which has replaced the mess of third graders and 26 flashcards!
